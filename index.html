About the Laboratory

The Data Mining Laboratory (DML) at the School of Computer Engineering, Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST), is an academic research group established in 2005 under the direction of Prof. Behrouz Minaei-Bidgoli. Our laboratory focuses on foundational and applied research at the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs), Knowledge Graphs, Information Extraction, and Recommender Systems.

Our research initiatives include the creation of FarsBase (the first comprehensive Persian knowledge graph deployed in search engines), FAIR-RAG and FARSIQA (faithful retrieval-augmented generation frameworks), and foundational benchmark datasets for low-resource Persian and Arabic language processing (PersianMHQA, Pars-ABSA, ElmNet, NoorGhateh).

Core Research Themes

Full Details & Active Student Topics →

1. Large Language Models & Faithful RAG

Mitigating hallucinations in complex domain-specific tasks via iterative refinement, verification loops (FAIR-RAG, FARSIQA), and multi-hop question answering (PersianMHQA, Bactrainus).

2. Knowledge Graphs & Semantic Reasoning

Constructing, expanding, and querying large-scale knowledge bases (FarsBase), link prediction with sentence embeddings (BGE, TransE), and neuro-symbolic reasoning (Borhan).

3. Recommender Systems & Social Networks

Addressing cold-start and sparsity problems in collaborative filtering through hybrid semantic embeddings, fuzzy clustering ensembles, and influence maximization.

4. Digital Humanities & Classical Corpora

Developing foundational corpora and morphological tools for historical, legal, and classical Arabic/Persian literature (Rezwan 1.2M corpus, NoorGhateh benchmark).
