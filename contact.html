Visiting via Public Transit:

The IUST campus is directly accessible via the Tehran Metro Line 2 (Dark Blue) at Elm-o-San'at University Metro Station. Exit the station onto Daneshgah Street and walk approximately 200 meters east to the campus main entrance.

Campus Vehicle Entry:

Vehicles should enter via the North Gate located on Hengam Street. Visitor parking is permitted upon clearance at the security reception.
