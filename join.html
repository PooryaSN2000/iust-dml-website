Prospective Students & Applications

Information for graduate applicants (Ph.D. and M.Sc.) and undergraduate interns seeking research supervision at DML.

Prospective Students

We welcome enthusiastic, motivated students who are eager to learn and explore research in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data mining.

Prior research experience is not strictly required. What matters most is a solid grasp of core computer science and mathematical fundamentals, genuine curiosity, and a willingness to learn.

How to Apply

If you are interested in conducting research or pursuing your thesis at DML, please send an email directly to Prof. Behrouz Minaei-Bidgoli at b_minaei@iust.ac.ir:

Email Subject Line Template:
Subject: [DML Prospective Student] [Ph.D. / M.Sc. / Intern] Your Full Name – [Research Topic or Area]
Example: [DML Prospective Student] [M.Sc.] Ali Rezai – LLM Reasoning & Preference Optimization

Please attach your CV / resume (PDF format) and include a brief note introducing your academic background and the specific research topics or directions you are interested in exploring.
