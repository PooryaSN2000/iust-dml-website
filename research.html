Research & Projects

DML investigates foundational algorithms and software architectures across Large Language Models (LLMs), Knowledge Graphs, Information Retrieval, and Recommender Systems. Below is a unified view of our core research directions, ongoing student thesis topics, and landmark open-source systems.

Core Research Directions

Theoretical and experimental frameworks established at the laboratory.

01. Natural Language Processing & LLMs

Faithful RAG, Reasoning & Multi-Hop QA

Mitigating hallucinations and multi-step reasoning failures in foundation models. We build iterative refinement loops (FAIR-RAG, FARSIQA) and multi-hop question answering benchmarks (PersianMHQA in ACM TALIP, Bactrainus) enforcing deductive chain validation.

Key Focus: Preference Optimization • Hallucination Mitigation • Dense Retrieval • Chain-of-Thought (CoT)
02. Knowledge Representation

Knowledge Graphs & Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning

Constructing and reasoning over structured ontological bases. Home of FarsBase (100M+ RDF triples). We investigate sentence embedding link prediction with BGE (published in Expert Systems with Applications), ontology alignment, and prioritized default logic engines (Borhan).

Key Focus: FarsBase • Unsupervised Entity Alignment • KG Compression • Default Logic
03. Information Systems & Data Mining

Recommender Systems & Graph Analytics

Overcoming sparsity and the cold-start barrier in recommendation algorithms. We develop semantic profile expansion, fuzzy cluster ensembles, and memory-efficient data structures for massive scale-free network generation (Ball*-tree in ACM SIGMOD).

Key Focus: Collaborative Filtering • Cluster Stability • Influence Maximization • Graph Synthesis
04. Computational Linguistics & Corpora

Digital Humanities & Low-Resource Benchmarks

Standardizing resources for low-resource and classical Persian and Arabic literature. Initiated the Rezwan 1.2M narrative hadith corpus, NoorGhateh morphological segmentation benchmark, and PESTS cross-lingual semantic similarity datasets.

Key Focus: Classical Morphology • Semantic Textual Similarity • Fact Verification • Virastyar
